the bracketed scene numbers (1/8–8/8) are your location cues if you drift.
Pace: unhurried, ~140 words/minute. Lines in *italics* are delivery notes.

## 0:00–0:04 — Title card

*Let the card breathe. Start speaking as the archive fades in.*

## 0:04–0:29 — 1/8 · A digital archive (25s)

"This is the Nepal Conflict Timeline — a digital archive of Nepal's internal
armed conflict, from November 1995 to September 2025. [pause] Over 2,100
events, compiled for transitional-justice research — and everything you're
about to see is live in the browser, driven by a single chronological feed."

*Cursor sweeps down to the histogram as the overview highlights.*

## 0:29–1:09 — 2/8 · One card at a time (40s)

"The archive reads year by year, month by month. Each card opens with a date
stack and a short teaser. [pause] Expanding a card reveals the full narrative
plus every metadata pill — actors, categories, places. This 2009 entry carries
eleven pills, so nothing is hidden behind a second click."

*Zoom settles on the card; click lands exactly on 'Show more'.*

## 1:09–1:49 — 3/8 · Search with typeahead (40s)

"Say you're researching Rolpa. Type two letters and the typeahead already
suggests Event IDs, districts, tags, and years. [pause] Hit Enter and the feed
narrows to thirty matching events — every hit highlighted — and you can step
through them one by one. Clearing the box restores the full archive."

*Let the typing finish before you mention the count; gesture at 1/30.*

## 1:49–2:37 — 4/8 · Filters drawer (48s)

"For deeper cuts, one Filters drawer holds everything. Theme chips grouped by
facet — actions, actors, policy, institutions. [pause] Categories,
organizations, newest-first sorting, and an exact date range. [pause] Notice
the address bar: every filter lives in the URL, so any view is a shareable
link."

*Pause a full beat on the URL point — it's the takeaway.*

## 2:37–3:12 — 5/8 · Jump by year (35s)

"The density bars show where the archive is thickest — the mid-2000s carry the
weight. [pause] Click any bar, scrub the year rail, or paste a year link: all
three land you in the same place. Here we jump from 2001 to 2002, then ride
the rail to 2005."

*Follow the cursor; don't narrate over the scrub motion.*

## 3:12–3:44 — 6/8 · Districts, permalinks, export (32s)

"Districts are inferred from the text across all 75 legacy districts, ranked
right here — one click filters the feed. [pause] Every card has its own
permalink that never carries your filters, so a link always opens. And any
view — filtered or full — downloads as CSV or JSON for researchers."

*Point at the toast when the link copies; linger on the export buttons.*

## 3:44–4:09 — 7/8 · Reading-first (25s)

"A word on care: this material is heavy. The toolbar offers larger text, high
contrast, and reduced graphic detail — and your choices persist. [pause]
System, light, or dark — the archive meets you where you are."

*Slow down here; this is the humane beat of the tour.*

## 4:09–4:24 — 8/8 · Open data, open code (15s)

"The data mirrors as open JSON for machines; the code is MIT. Event texts keep
their sheet provenance — the archive documents its sources rather than
laundering them."

## 4:24–4:30 — Outro card
